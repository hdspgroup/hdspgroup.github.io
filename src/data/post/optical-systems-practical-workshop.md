This activity was organized by the HDSP research seedbed (semillero 4765) as part of the **Forum on Computational Optics and Deep Learning: Applications and Challenges**, a cycle of four sessions held during the 2026-2 academic semester. The forum was free and open to students from any program at UIS and to anyone interested in research, as part of the seedbed's solidarity outreach project under the 2026 Strengthening Plan of the Vice-Rectorate for Research and Extension (VIE).

The third activity of the forum was hands-on. It began with an introduction to the **optical devices** available in the laboratory and an explanation of how each experimental component works and what it is used for.

Participants then **assembled a basic optical system** on their own. With the setup ready, they **acquired and processed real data and signals** coming from their own experimental system.

This was where the theory from the previous workshop met practice: align, measure, check, and measure again, using the same methodologies applied in a research project.

- Optical devices available in the laboratory
- Operation and applications of experimental components
- Assembly of a basic optical system by the participants
- Acquisition and processing of real data and signals
- Integration of theory and experimental practice
