This activity was organized by the HDSP research seedbed (semillero 4765) as part of the **Forum on Computational Optics and Deep Learning: Applications and Challenges**, a cycle of four sessions held during the 2026-2 academic semester. The forum was free and open to students from any program at UIS and to anyone interested in research, as part of the seedbed's solidarity outreach project under the 2026 Strengthening Plan of the Vice-Rectorate for Research and Extension (VIE).

The forum closed with a keynote talk. It started from the **theoretical foundations of light** and its role in today's science and technology, and moved toward the relationship between **optics, image processing, and artificial intelligence**.

The talk presented applications across different industries built with **deep learning** tools, and discussed how combining light and AI is opening new frontiers in scientific and technological research, and why computational optics helps solve complex problems.

The session ended with an open space for questions and discussion between students and researchers, and with an invitation to join the training processes of the seedbed and the research group.
