This activity was organized by the HDSP research seedbed (semillero 4765) as part of the **Forum on Computational Optics and Deep Learning: Applications and Challenges**, a cycle of four sessions held during the 2026-2 academic semester. The forum was free and open to students from any program at UIS and to anyone interested in research, as part of the seedbed's solidarity outreach project under the 2026 Strengthening Plan of the Vice-Rectorate for Research and Extension (VIE).

The first activity of the forum took place inside the HDSP optics laboratory. Attendees toured the facilities and saw up close the equipment the group has used in its projects: what each instrument does, how it combines with the others, and which research it has supported.

The tour introduced the laboratory's **research lines** and some **representative projects** that bring together optical instrumentation, image processing, and deep learning. With those examples in view, the discussion covered how each setup made it possible to tackle a concrete scientific or technological problem.

The visit closed with a reflection on the role of **experimental infrastructure** in producing knowledge, and on the opportunities the laboratory offers to anyone who wants to start doing research as an undergraduate.
